Refund policy

Terms for cancellations and refunds.

Refunds

All refunds for bookings are at no cost until seven days after the booking date. Seven days or more after the booking date any cancellations will incur a 3% charge.

We will notify you once we've received and inspected your return, and let you know if the refund was approved or not. If approved, you'll be automatically refunded on your original payment method within 10 business days. Please remember it can take some time for your bank or credit card company to process and post the refund too.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we've approved your return, please contact us at mbrauser@joyridedelray.com.
